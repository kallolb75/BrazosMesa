# S3: The model is the part you can rent

Substack, Rewire the Enterprise, Thu Oct 15, 2026, 5:00 PM CT.

*And why the part around it should be yours.*

Some good news to start. You don't have to build a model. Several very good ones are available to rent, and they keep improving.

What makes a model useful inside your company is the layer wrapped around it. I think of it in four pieces:

- **Context.** What it can see: your documents, your data, the right slice of both.
- **Tools.** What it can do: look something up, file a ticket, update a record.
- **Guardrails.** What it may not do, and when a person has to approve.
- **Evidence.** How you know it worked: logs, traces and tests.

That wrapper is the harness.

![A rented model in the middle, ringed by four pieces the enterprise owns: context, tools, guardrails and evidence. Together they are the harness.](../images/S3_the-part-you-can-rent.png)

Here's why ownership matters. You'll probably swap the model at least once. The harness holds your data, your rules and everything you learn along the way, so it stays put when the model changes.

Partners can help you build it. The wheel stays with you.

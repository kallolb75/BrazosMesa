# S2: Same model, different Tuesday

Substack, Rewire the Enterprise, Wed Oct 14, 2026, 5:00 PM CT.

*Why AI demos go well and first projects stall.*

I've sat through a lot of AI demos this year. They all go well. The model gets one clean table and a question it was built to answer.

Then someone asks it about real data. Real data lives in the layers from the first post: the new app, the 2011 integration everyone is afraid to touch, three copies of the truth, and the spreadsheet holding it up.

![Two pictures. In the demo, the model connects to one clean table. On your actual Tuesday, the same model is wired to a new app, a 2011 integration, three data platforms and a spreadsheet.](../images/S2_same-model-different-tuesday.png)

The model is the same in both pictures. The difference is the plumbing between it and your systems. Someone has to get it to the right data with permission to use it, and someone has to record what it did.

Demos skip that part. Your first real project will spend most of its time there.

Tomorrow: which part of this you can rent.

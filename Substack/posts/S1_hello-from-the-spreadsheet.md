# S1: Hello from the spreadsheet holding up your company

Substack, Rewire the Enterprise, first post.

*A short introduction to Rewire the Enterprise, and what you'll find here.*

![Tangled wires on the left straighten into four neat lines labelled apps, integration, data and the spreadsheet.](../images/S1_cover.png)

Every large organization I've worked with has one: a spreadsheet on a shared drive that something important depends on. The person who built it left years ago, and everyone is a little afraid to close it.

I've spent 25 years in the layers under that spreadsheet: custom applications, the integrations between them, and the data platforms they all lean on. Most modernization projects succeed or fail in those layers, so that's what I'm going to write about here.

![A stack of layers. The new app, launched last quarter. The integrations, built in 2011. The data platform, three copies of the truth. All of it resting on one small spreadsheet.](../images/S1_your-enterprise-honestly.png)

## What you'll find here

**Essays on modernization.** How big organizations decide what to keep and what to rebuild, including the calls I got wrong.

**Builds, written up as they happen.** Now and then I'll build something and show the work, including the parts that broke.

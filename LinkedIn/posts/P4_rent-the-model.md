# P4: Rent the model. Build the harness yourself.

LinkedIn, Tue Oct 13, 2026. Image: [P4_rent-the-model.png](../infographics/P4_rent-the-model.png)

Last week: use the AI, stay accountable for what ships. This week: what's worth building.

Every enterprise rents the same models. GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, by the token. The model can't tell you apart from your competitor, because they're renting it too.

What's yours is the layer around it:

1. Context: your data, your rules, what lives in people's heads.
2. Orchestration: which agent runs, which tool it calls, which model answers.
3. Governance: policy, risk, who approved, what got logged.
4. Execution: the people, the systems, the write-back.

Those four around the rented model are the Enterprise AI Harness. Rent the model. Build the harness yourself.

Yourself means in the driver's seat. Partners can help with the build. The wheel stays with the enterprise, because whoever builds the harness learns the business: the context, the decisions, the audit trail. And your apps and data are about to be operated by agents. The harness is what makes that safe. It's modernization's next integration layer.

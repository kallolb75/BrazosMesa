# P5: You already have a harness. You just don't own it.

LinkedIn, Thu Oct 15, 2026. Image: [P5_the-gap.png](../infographics/P5_the-gap.png)

On Tuesday I said what's worth building: the Enterprise AI Harness, the layer between a rented model and your systems.

Here's why it isn't optional.

Roughly 8 in 10 large enterprises run a model in production today. Roughly 1 in 4 treat the context it runs on as something they own.

The other three in four still have a harness. Every copilot, every chatbot, every pilot that reached production built one by accident. Context is whatever got pasted into the prompt. Orchestration is whatever glue code calls the model. Governance is whoever approved it, usually nobody in writing.

Where it leaks:

1. A vendor's platform. Your ontologies and workflow logic, encoded in someone else's product.
2. A departing employee. The judgment that never got written down leaves on their last day.
3. An unlogged run. A decision was made. Nobody can replay it. The regulator will ask.

That's the gap. You have a harness. The question is whether you own it.

Owning it means four things: the context, the workflow logic, the telemetry, and the right to swap the model.

Saturday: the four pieces to build first, in order.

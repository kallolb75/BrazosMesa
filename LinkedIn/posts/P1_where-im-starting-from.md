# P1: Where I'm starting from

LinkedIn, Wed Oct 7, 2026. Image: [P1_where-im-starting-from.png](../infographics/P1_where-im-starting-from.png)

I wrote code for a living until 2014. In the 12 years since, close to none.

Like a lot of engineers who move up, my work moved to the business problem: stakeholders, functional SMEs, what the organization needs.

My job became the spec, the design and the architecture. The code got built somewhere else. When it came back, I didn't open it.

That works until someone in the room asks about a specific line. I can defend the design. I can't defend the code.

This series is about closing that gap.
